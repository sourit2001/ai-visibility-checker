# Mentioned design (2026-10-31)
Idea: the yellow highlighter mark on an AI answer = "you got mentioned". The hero headline is a fill-in-the-blanks sentence ("Someone asks ChatGPT for the best ___ for ___. Does it name ___?") whose blanks are the form inputs; the brand blank is highlighted yellow.
Palette: paper #f3f5fa, sheet #ffffff, ink navy #1b2340, slate #5a6378, rule #d8deea, highlighter #ffe45c (deep #f2cc12), miss #b8433a. Check section is ink-navy with yellow score.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display), IBM Plex Sans (body). Sentence case, no all-caps labels.
Layout: left-aligned; desktop hero splits sentence + tilted sample answer; mobile stacks. Reduced motion respected. Source: dist/index.html, dist/style.css, dist/app.js.
